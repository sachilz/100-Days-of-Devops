# 🚀 100 Days of DevOps - KodeCloud

Welcome to my personal tracking repository for the **KodeCloud 100 Days of DevOps** course!

## 📌 What is this repository?
This repository is dedicated to documenting my learning journey through the 100 Days of DevOps challenge by KodeCloud. It contains all the questions, exercises, and concepts covered during the course, along with my detailed answers and notes.

## 🎯 Why did I create it?
The main goal of this repository is to serve as a comprehensive knowledge base. By manually documenting each question and answer clearly, I aim to:
- **Understand concepts properly:** Writing things down solidifies my understanding of complex DevOps topics.
- **Create a personal reference guide:** It acts as a quick-reference guide I can look back on for future projects or interview preparation.
- **Track my progress:** It helps me stay accountable and visualize my day-to-day growth in the DevOps field.

## 📁 Repository Structure
To keep things organized and easy to navigate, the 100 questions are broken down into 10 logical folders, each containing 10 questions:

- 📂 `Questions_01_to_10/`
- 📂 `Questions_11_to_20/`
- 📂 `...`
- 📂 `Questions_91_to_100/`

Inside each folder, you will find individual markdown files (e.g., `Question_001.md`) containing the specific question and its corresponding answer.
